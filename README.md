# SRT/VTT Cleaner & IRaMuTeQ Corpus Formatter (FR) — Obsidian & Zotero SHS

Free, zero-dependency, 100% offline **Obsidian Community Plugin** (`MIT`) for qualitative social science researchers (CNRS, universities, PhD candidates using **Zotero**, **IRaMuTeQ**, **MAXQDA**, or **NVivo**), journalists, and committee secretaries.

## Features (100% Offline, Zero Network Calls)
1. **Clean `.SRT` / `.VTT` subtitles (Teams, Zoom, YouTube, Whisper)** into readable Markdown interview turns with merged consecutive speaker lines and preserved initial `[hh:mm:ss]` timecodes.
2. **One-click `IRaMuTeQ` / `TXM` starred corpus export** (`**** *entretien` and `-*loc_speaker`, stripping forbidden `*` inside verbatim text).
3. **French typographic normalization** (`\u202F` narrow no-break spaces before `: ; ? ! %` and `\u00A0` around `« … »` and `€`).
4. **Zotero Literature & Interview Note workflow**: pair your Zotero `@citekey` YAML frontmatter in Obsidian with clean, timecoded qualitative verbatims.
